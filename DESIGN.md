# Design language

**2026-10-16 — PAPER TEAR (current).** Owner pivoted from SIGNAL: "make the theme paper tear... white theme... bright Gen Z colors." Full reskin to a torn-paper zine/collage aesthetic, matching his opportunity-reels channel identity (torn paper, hand-drawn arrows, red/pink accents).

- **Palette:** warm paper `#FAF6EE`, white sticker cards, ink `#1B1712`; Gen Z brights — risk red `#FF3B30`, hot pink `#FF4D8D`, highlighter `#FFD02F`, lime `#A3E635`, sky `#38BDF8`, grape `#8B5CF6`.
- **Typography:** Archivo 900 (chunky display/statements), JetBrains Mono (labels/data), Caveat (handwritten annotations), Noto Sans (Devanagari).
- **Signature components:** sticker cards (2px ink border, hard offset shadow, slight rotations), washi tape strips, highlighter swipes behind key words, torn-edge clip-path dividers, doodle stars/arrows, rotated flag callouts.
- **Motion:** the SIGNAL motion system carries over (char-split hero, scramble eyebrows, scrubbed word-fill statements, scanline wipe chapter, magnetic CTAs, velocity marquees), restyled for paper.
- **Dashboard:** paper stickers, ink rail with yellow active states, bright charts; fixed shell layout retained.
- **Rule:** UI only. No `apps/api` or `apps/ml` changes; every route, form, API call, auth action, and data contract unchanged.
